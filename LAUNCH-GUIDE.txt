FREE HOSTING WITH CLOUDFLARE PAGES
1. Sign in to Cloudflare.
2. Open Workers & Pages.
3. Choose Create application > Pages > Direct Upload.
4. Upload this entire unzipped folder.
5. Deploy.
6. In the Pages project, open Custom domains.
7. Add fr55bel.media.co.uk.
8. Follow Cloudflare's DNS instructions.
